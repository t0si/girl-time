Privacy Policy for girl time

Last updated: September 2026

girl time is a small personal scheduling tool that helps you plan backward from an event to figure out when to start getting ready. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

If you use the app without signing in, your events, steps, and saved routines are stored only in your own browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere, and we never see it.

If you choose to sign in (with Google or an email link) to sync your data across devices, we store:

  • Your email address and basic profile info (name, profile picture) provided by Google or your email sign-in, used only to identify your account
  • The event names, times, steps, and saved routines you create in the app, tied to your account

How it's stored

Signed-in data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), a third-party service we use to run the app. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than making the app work for you. There are no ads in this app.

Your data, your control

You can stop syncing at any time by signing out — your data will stay in your account for next time, but nothing further will sync from that device until you sign in again. If you'd like your account data deleted entirely, contact us using the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the app's creator, Marina, via the contact info associated with this project's GitHub repository.

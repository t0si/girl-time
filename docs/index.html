Values sort exercise

A quiet way to see what matters most to you right now.

  1. Select a card below, then choose where it belongs: not important, somewhat important, important, or very important.
  2. Sort at your own pace — there's no right answer, just what feels true for you today.
  3. Use “Reset all cards” at any time to start over.
  4. When you're done, use the space near the bottom to reflect.

This sort lives only in your browser tab — nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and reloading the page resets it.

Let's reflect

Look at your “important” and “very important” columns. What do you notice? Write freely below, or start from these prompts:

  • Which values surprised you?
  • Where does your life already reflect these values?
  • Where is there a gap between what you value and how you spend your time?

Exercise credit: W.R. Miller, J. C’de Baca, D.B. Matthews, P.L. Wilbourne — University of New Mexico, 2001
